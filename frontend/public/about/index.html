Meet your race intelligence workspace.

FrontWing is a Formula 1 analysis platform built by Pranav Attarde, a full stack developer, AI engineer and motorsport enthusiast in Pune, India.

What can you do with FrontWing?

  • Ask race questions in the briefing room and inspect supporting evidence.
  • Explore pit windows, tyre degradation and what-if scenarios in the Strategy Engineer.
  • Compare racing laps through the 3D Ghost Battle experience.

From data to explanation

The project combines FastF1 telemetry workflows, AI orchestration, backend services and a React interface. Python, FastAPI and LangGraph support analysis, with PostgreSQL and Redis in the wider system.

Data availability depends on the race and session. Simulations depend on their inputs and assumptions; an AI explanation should be checked against the underlying evidence. FrontWing is an independent project and is not affiliated with Formula 1 or its teams.
